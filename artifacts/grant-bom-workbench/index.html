What this tool does and does not verify

Enter actual supplier quotes yourself. Prices are not fetched or verified. A budget under a program cap is not approval. No manufacturing, human work hours, grant payment or shipping is claimed.

The default cap is the published USD 35 limit for the 8-Pin Grant. PCB design, KiCad checks, an honest journal and organizer approval are separate requirements.
